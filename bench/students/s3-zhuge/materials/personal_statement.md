# Personal Statement (final version)

For four years I have written the code that sits closest to the motor. At Lanhai Semiconductor I wrote driver firmware and a bootloader that shipped on three product lines; at Qiyuan I now lead firmware for a collaborative robot joint and cut the torque-loop latency from 1.2 ms to 0.4 ms. The work has been rewarding, but it has also shown me where my training stops. I can tune a controller, yet I cannot prove it is stable when the payload changes; I can filter a noisy encoder, yet I choose the filter by habit rather than by theory. I am applying for a master's degree to rebuild those foundations - modern control, estimation and robot dynamics - and to test them on real hardware. My goal is to return to industry able to design motion controllers that are safe by construction, not by luck.

Area of interest / proposed topic: Low-latency torque control for collaborative robot joints
