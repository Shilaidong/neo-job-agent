# Personal statement (final)

Two years of forecasting retail demand have shown me both the power and the limits of the statistics I learned at Manchester. My models work — one hierarchical time-series model cut our forecast error by 11% — but when a client asks “how sure are you?” I often cannot give an honest answer. I want to return to study to close that gap. My undergraduate project on shrinkage estimators, supervised by Professor Whitcombe, and the LSE summer course in machine learning gave me the foundations; daily work with messy, sparse sales data gave me the motivation. I am particularly interested in Bayesian hierarchical models and in uncertainty quantification for decisions made under time pressure. After the programme I intend to return to industry in Hong Kong, and to build forecasting tools whose stated confidence can actually be trusted.

Proposed topic / area of interest: Hierarchical Bayesian forecasting for sparse retail demand
